Technical Specifications

Dual-Layer Security Infrastructure

Prompt Security Architecture

A dual-layer prompt injection detection system designed to intercept and evaluate prompts before they reach a vulnerable Large Language Model. Securing the AI boundary against instruction overrides, role-playing, and contextual hijacking.

Detection Engines

  • Rule-Based Analysis: Identifies explicit instruction overrides, role-playing prefixes, encoding evasion, and known prompt injection signatures in real-time.
  • DistilBERT Classifier: Analyzes the semantic intent of the payload, trained on over 45,000 benign and malicious prompts to detect zero-day and novel linguistic attacks.

Risk Evaluation & Decisions

The system evaluates threats across a robust probability matrix:

LOW MEDIUM HIGH CRITICAL

Determining whether a payload is permitted, flagged for review, or immediately blocked from the LLM execution pipeline.
